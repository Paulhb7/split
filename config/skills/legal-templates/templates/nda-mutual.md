# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Effective Date:** {EFFECTIVE_DATE}

**Between**
{PARTY_A_NAME}, {PARTY_A_REGISTRATION}, having its registered office at {PARTY_A_ADDRESS} ("**Party A**")
**and**
{PARTY_B_NAME}, {PARTY_B_REGISTRATION}, having its registered office at {PARTY_B_ADDRESS} ("**Party B**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange Confidential Information in connection with: {PURPOSE} (the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement governs the disclosure and use of Confidential Information exchanged for the Purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other (the "**Receiving Party**") in any form (oral, written, electronic, visual), whether or not marked confidential, that a reasonable person would consider confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation: business plans, customer lists, financial information, source code, technical specifications, product roadmaps, and personal data of the Disclosing Party's employees and customers.

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
(b) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records;
(c) is rightfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality;
(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information.

## 3. Use and Protection

The Receiving Party shall:
(a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees, affiliates, and professional advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement;
(c) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than reasonable care;
(d) promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any actual or suspected unauthorised disclosure.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law, court order, or competent authority to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, notify the Disclosing Party in advance to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order, and shall disclose only the minimum necessary.

## 5. No License, No Warranty

Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any right or licence in the Confidential Information of the other, except the limited right to use it for the Purpose. Confidential Information is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind.

## 6. Term and Survival

This Agreement enters into force on the Effective Date and remains in effect for {TERM_MONTHS} months. The confidentiality obligations in Sections 2 to 5 survive termination of this Agreement for an additional {TERM_MONTHS} months.

## 7. Return or Destruction (optional)

[OPTIONAL — return_of_materials]
Upon termination of this Agreement or upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession (including copies) and certify such destruction in writing.

## 8. Non-Solicitation (optional)

[OPTIONAL — non_solicitation]
For twelve (12) months following the Effective Date, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose, except through general public advertising.

## 9. No Publicity (optional)

[OPTIONAL — no_publicity]
Neither Party shall disclose the existence or content of the discussions covered by the Purpose, or use the other Party's name or marks in any publicity, without the prior written consent of the other Party.

## 10. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by {GOVERNING_LAW}. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the {JURISDICTION_VENUE}.

## 11. Miscellaneous

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding of the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes any prior oral or written communications. No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder remains in force.
