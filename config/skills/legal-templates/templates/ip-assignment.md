# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

**Effective Date:** {EFFECTIVE_DATE}

**Between**
{ASSIGNOR_NAME}, an individual / entity residing at {ASSIGNOR_ADDRESS} ("**Assignor**")
**and**
{ASSIGNEE_NAME}, {ASSIGNEE_REGISTRATION}, with registered office at {ASSIGNEE_ADDRESS} ("**Assignee**").

## 1. Definitions

"**Intellectual Property**" or "**IP**" means all intellectual and industrial property rights of any kind in any jurisdiction, including, without limitation: copyrights and related rights, patents and patent applications, trademarks and service marks (whether registered or not), trade secrets, know-how, design rights, database rights, domain names, and all rights to apply for, register, prosecute, defend, and enforce any of the foregoing, together with all renewals, extensions, divisionals, continuations, reissues, and reissues thereof.

"**Assigned IP**" means the IP described in Schedule A: {IP_DEFINITION}, together with all IP rights in derivative works, improvements, and related materials created by Assignor up to the Effective Date that arise out of or relate to the same subject matter.

## 2. Assignment

In consideration of {CONSIDERATION}, the sufficiency and receipt of which Assignor expressly acknowledges, Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to Assignee, free and clear of all liens and encumbrances, all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the Assigned IP, on a worldwide and exclusive basis, for the full duration of such rights as protected under the laws of each jurisdiction and for all known and unknown modes of exploitation.

The assignment includes, without limitation, the right to:
(a) reproduce, distribute, publicly perform, display, and communicate to the public;
(b) adapt, modify, translate, and create derivative works;
(c) sublicense to any third party on any terms;
(d) register, prosecute, and enforce the Assigned IP in any jurisdiction;
(e) assign the Assigned IP to any successor in interest.

## 3. Moral Rights (optional)

[OPTIONAL — moral_rights_waiver]
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Assignor waives all moral rights (*droits moraux*, *Persönlichkeitsrechte*, paternity, integrity, disclosure, withdrawal) in the Assigned IP. Where waiver is not permitted (notably in France for the *droit moral* attached to certain works), Assignor undertakes not to exercise such rights in a manner that would prevent, restrict, or impair Assignee's exploitation of the Assigned IP as contemplated by this Agreement.

## 4. Representations and Warranties (optional)

[OPTIONAL — representations]
Assignor represents and warrants that:
(a) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of the Assigned IP;
(b) the Assigned IP does not, to Assignor's knowledge, infringe any third-party right;
(c) Assignor has not previously assigned, licensed, or encumbered the Assigned IP in any way that conflicts with this assignment;
(d) Assignor has the full right, power, and authority to enter into this Agreement and perform the obligations herein.

## 5. Prior Inventions (optional)

[OPTIONAL — prior_inventions]
Notwithstanding Section 2, Assignor expressly excludes from the Assigned IP the prior inventions, works, and other intellectual property listed in Schedule B (the "**Prior Inventions**"). For the avoidance of doubt, no rights in the Prior Inventions are transferred to Assignee under this Agreement, except a non-exclusive licence to the extent any Prior Invention is necessarily incorporated into the Assigned IP and identified by Assignor in writing.

If Schedule B is omitted or empty, the Assigned IP is assigned in its entirety with no exclusion.

## 6. Further Assurances (optional)

[OPTIONAL — further_assurances]
Assignor shall, at Assignee's reasonable request and expense, sign any documents and take any further actions reasonably necessary or appropriate to vest, perfect, defend, or enforce Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP, including signing patent or trademark applications and assignments, and giving testimony in legal proceedings.

## 7. Consideration

The consideration described in Section 2 is the entire consideration for this assignment. Assignor acknowledges that this consideration is full and final.

## 8. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by {GOVERNING_LAW}. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the {JURISDICTION_VENUE}.

## 9. Miscellaneous

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter. It supersedes any prior agreement on the same subject. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect.
