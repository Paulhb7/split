## 5. Acceptance (optional)

Client has fifteen (15) business days from receipt of each Deliverable to review it and notify Provider in writing of any non-conformity, with reasonable specificity. If no notice is given within that period, the Deliverable is deemed accepted. Provider shall have a reasonable period to correct properly notified non-conformities.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Upon full payment of the fees, Provider assigns to Client, on a worldwide, exclusive, and irrevocable basis, all intellectual property rights in the Deliverables specifically created for Client under this Agreement, for the maximum legal duration permitted in the relevant jurisdictions and for all known modes of exploitation. The assignment includes the right to reproduce, adapt, translate, publicly perform, distribute, and create derivative works.

Provider retains all rights in:
(a) any pre-existing materials, methodologies, tools, frameworks, or know-how Provider owned or developed prior to or independently of this Agreement; and
(b) generic skills, experience, and unaided memory.

To the extent Provider's pre-existing materials are incorporated into the Deliverables, Provider grants Client a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use, reproduce, and adapt them solely as embedded in the Deliverables.

Provider may, with Client's prior written consent (not unreasonably withheld), include the Deliverables in its portfolio for self-promotion.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each Party shall keep confidential any non-public information of the other received under or in connection with this Agreement, use it only for performance of this Agreement, and protect it with reasonable care. This obligation survives termination for three (3) years.

## 8. Liability

Each Party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement, regardless of the form of action, shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by Client to Provider during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to liability. Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, incidental, special, or punitive damages, including lost profits or lost data, except in cases of gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality.

## 9. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement:
(a) for convenience, on thirty (30) days prior written notice;
(b) for material breach not cured within fifteen (15) days of written notice describing the breach.

On termination, Client pays Provider for Services performed and Deliverables completed through the effective termination date.
