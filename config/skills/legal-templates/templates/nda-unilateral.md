# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Effective Date:** {EFFECTIVE_DATE}

**Disclosing Party:** {DISCLOSER_NAME}, {DISCLOSER_REGISTRATION}, {DISCLOSER_ADDRESS} ("**Discloser**")

**Receiving Party:** {RECIPIENT_NAME}, {RECIPIENT_REGISTRATION}, {RECIPIENT_ADDRESS} ("**Recipient**")

## 1. Purpose

Discloser will share certain Confidential Information with Recipient for the purpose of: {PURPOSE} (the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement governs Recipient's use of such information.

## 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient in any form, whether marked confidential or not, that a reasonable person would treat as confidential given its nature. It includes, without limitation, business plans, technology, financials, customer data, product roadmaps, pitch materials, and source code.

It does **not** include information that:
(a) is or becomes public other than through Recipient's breach;
(b) was known to Recipient prior to disclosure, as documented;
(c) is received from a third party without confidentiality obligation;
(d) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of Recipient

Recipient shall:
(a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b) not disclose it to any third party except advisors bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations and on a need-to-know basis;
(c) safeguard it with reasonable care;
(d) promptly notify Discloser of any actual or suspected unauthorised disclosure.

## 4. Compelled Disclosure

If Recipient is legally required to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall, where lawful, give Discloser prompt prior notice to allow protective measures, and shall disclose only the minimum required.

## 5. Residuals (optional)

[OPTIONAL — residuals]
Notwithstanding the foregoing, Recipient may use, for any purpose, general knowledge, skills, and experience retained in unaided memory by individuals who had access to Confidential Information, provided that this clause does not authorise disclosure or use of Discloser's specific Confidential Information.

## 6. No Licence

Discloser retains all rights in Confidential Information. No licence is granted by implication or otherwise.

## 7. Term

This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and remains in force for {TERM_MONTHS} months. Recipient's confidentiality obligations survive termination for an additional {TERM_MONTHS} months.

## 8. Non-Solicitation (optional)

[OPTIONAL — non_solicitation]
For twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, Recipient shall not solicit for employment any employee of Discloser with whom Recipient had contact in connection with the Purpose, except through general public advertising.

## 9. No Publicity (optional)

[OPTIONAL — no_publicity]
Recipient shall not announce, publish, or otherwise disclose the existence or content of the discussions covered by the Purpose, or use Discloser's name or marks in any publicity, without Discloser's prior written consent.

## 10. Remedies

Recipient acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to Discloser for which monetary damages may be inadequate. Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedy.

## 11. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by {GOVERNING_LAW}. Any dispute shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the {JURISDICTION_VENUE}.

## 12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire understanding of the Parties regarding its subject matter. No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
