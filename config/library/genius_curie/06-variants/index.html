About this — why this exists, and how to read it ▸

Why

To show, at a glance, the pathogenic-variant landscape of a well-characterised orphan disease along its causal protein — where mutations cluster, and which single allele carries the disease.

Context

Cystic fibrosis (~1 in 2,500–3,500 newborns of European descent) is caused by loss-of-function variants in CFTR, an ABC-transporter chloride channel. Over 1,000 variants are now classified as disease-causing.

How to use it

Hover a needle for its HGVS, consequence, and clinical significance. Filter by significance with the chips. The domain track and hotspot bands are drawn to residue scale.

What I assumed

A curated, teaching-grade panel of well-documented variants (not the full ClinVar dump). Heights encode reported allele frequency in CF cohorts, not ClinVar submission counts. Domain limits follow UniProt features.

NBD1 is the hotspot. The recurrent in-frame deletion p.Phe508del (a misfolding, class II defect) and the gating/processing cluster around 542·551·553·560 all sit in the first nucleotide-binding domain. Removing one residue at 508 destabilises folding enough to dominate the entire disease.
