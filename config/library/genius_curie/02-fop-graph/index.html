▶ About this map — why it exists, how to read it, what I assumed

Why

A curated biomedical knowledge graph — a PrimeKG-style subgraph — that walks the full arc of a single orphan disease: disease → gene → mutation → protein → pathway → biology → phenotype → drug, so the mechanism and the therapeutic pipeline are legible on one page.

Context

FOP (≈1 in 1,000,000) is driven by the recurrent ACVR1 R206H variant. The node set is hand-picked and each drug, target and trial verified against OMIM, UniProt, RCSB PDB, ClinicalTrials.gov and the primary literature (2013–2026).

How to use

Click any node → a cited explanation opens on the right with one real reference and its neighbours. Filter by class in the top-left, or hit “Causal path only” to isolate disease → drug. Drag nodes, scroll to zoom, search by name.

What I assumed

Classic R206H FOP (not the atypical variants). Drug status reflects the public record to July 2026 — incl. garetosmab’s positive phase-3 OPTIMA and the discontinued FALKON (fidrisertib). This is a research/teaching map, not medical advice.
