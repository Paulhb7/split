Two fields — the biology of fungal threads and the science of engineered materials — and the load-bearing analogies that let one grow the other.

About this piece

Why

A map of the strongest analogies between mycology and materials science — the idea that a fungus's branching body can be grown, tuned, and killed into a structural material.

Context

The mycelium-materials market was ~US$3.6 B in 2025 and is projected to reach ~US$7.9 B by 2035. Every dated claim, company, and paper here was verified against July-2026 sources; each node carries one real reference.
