About this tracker Why · Context · How · Assumed

Why

You committed to a half-marathon and asked me to coach the running and the eating together. This is the flagship of the merged Coach: one plan where fuel is woven into the training, proving a single coach who holds both domains beats four who each see half the picture.

Context

A 12-week periodised build from a ~25 km/week base to a sub-1:45 finish. Four runs a week (~4h), Sunday long run, deload weeks at 4 and 8, a two-week taper into race day. Fuelling scales to the week's load — carbs up around long runs, dialled back on rest.

How to use it

Open it daily. Tick this week's sessions as you run them (streak grows, volume chart fills). Each morning, read the fuel flag — it reacts to the day's training load — then log carbs and water with the pips. Everything saves on this device.

What I assumed

Base of a 48:30 10K, no allergies, ~2,300 kcal maintenance, Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun schedule. Paces are targets, not gospel. I coach plans, not medicine — real pain (not soreness) or any symptom means stop and see a physio or GP, not push through.
