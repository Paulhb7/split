Why this exists

You committed to Italian to A2 over one term. This handout is the plan and the daily instrument in one file — so twelve weeks from now the goal, the route, and your own progress are all still here, no memory of this conversation required.

Context

Zero starting point, roughly five hours a week (about 45 minutes a day). A2 means: introduce yourself, order, shop, describe your day, cope with a simple past. The route is twelve weekly modules, each a grammar spine + a vocabulary field + one tiny project to force output.

How to use it

Land here every day on the Dashboard (03): clear the review queue (flip a card, grade it honestly), tick the week's recall targets, log Friday's self-check. The Program (02) is reference — open a week when you sit down to study it.
