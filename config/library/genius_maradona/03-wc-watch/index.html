A
The attack that won't stop

Dembélé ripped a hat-trick past Norway; Mbappé leads the Boot on six and broke France's all-time men's scoring record with a laser past Mendy vs Senegal. Olise is the tournament's assist king. Three creators, one machine.

R
Route so far

Topped their group, then France 3–0 Sweden in the Round of 32. Next: Paraguay in the Round of 16 (4 Jul, Philadelphia) — the giant-killers who knocked out Germany on penalties. A banana-skin, on paper only.

WWWWW
Diego's read

They look like the team to beat. But a side that scores four every night can forget how to defend one. The football is a joy — I just want to see them in a real fight before I crown them.

CAPE

Population half a million. World Cup debutants. And now the smallest nation ever to reach the knockout stage in the tournament's 96-year history — the Blue Sharks did it with three draws and a wall in front of goal.
~525k
people — the smallest nation to ever make the World Cup knockouts

draws from three games — vs Spain, Uruguay & Saudi Arabia to finish 2nd

Vozinha, the 40-year-old wall

Their goalkeeper kept two clean sheets and made seven saves against Spain alone, becoming an overnight sensation. Diaspora-built too — six of the starting XI born abroad, from the Netherlands to Portugal.

R
And they're not alone

Africa is having a World Cup moment: Morocco leads all nations with seven goals and bounced the Netherlands on penalties. Colombia is the ranking's biggest climber (+5). The old order is being made to sweat.

Diego's read

This is why we watch. A nation the size of a town, holding the European champions, dreaming out loud. Next up: Messi's Argentina. The fairytale probably ends there — but nobody can take the group stage from them.

THE ONE-TWO
WITH WIRTZ
DENIZ UNDAV Germany · group stage
A goal you build, not blast. Undav played a delightful exchange of passes with Florian Wirtz — wall pass, drop, run — and then the unerring side-foot finish, placement over power. It's the one FIFA's fan vote keeps coming back to for Goal of the Tournament: not the hardest hit, the best made.

ALSO IN THE VOTE
MESSI · off De Paul

Argentina's No.10 collected a superb Rodrigo De Paul pass and found the top corner — the trademark left-foot bend that's been in his locker for twenty years.

MESSI · solo vs Algeria

The pick of his hat-trick: ball in midfield, dribble forward, onto the left foot, top corner. A one-man goal on opening night — vintage.

MBAPPÉ · vs Senegal

Not subtle — a laser past Édouard Mendy that set France's all-time men's scoring record. Pure violence, and it counts.
