Exhibit VI · The Case for the CrownVIII

Five arguments for buying the fear.

i.

The cheapest growth on the board

19.6× forward earnings for +71% trailing revenue growth. The multiple prices a deceleration that the order book — USD 1 trillion of Blackwell and Rubin demand through 2027, doubled in twelve months — flatly contradicts. [5][6]

ii.

The patrons keep raising

Combined hyperscaler capex guided to ≈ USD 725B for 2026, up 77%. NVIDIA captures the largest single share of every incremental dollar, and Rubin lands into that budget in H2 2026. [7][6]

iii.

A platform, not a part

NVL72 rack systems, networking attach, CUDA. Gross margin held at ≈ 75% through a full architecture transition — pricing power that survives its own product turnover is the definition of a moat. [1]

iv.

Capital returns turned real

Dividend raised twenty-five-fold, USD 80B of fresh buyback, ≈ USD 20B returned in a single quarter. The balance sheet has begun paying shareholders while still growing 85%. [1][3]

v.

China is a free option

Guidance embeds zero China data-center revenue. H200 is cleared for export at a 25% remit; any actual delivery is upside the model does not require. [1][12]

Exhibit VII · The Case AgainstIX

Five arguments the June sellers made.

i.

The capex-to-revenue gap

AI infrastructure spend is growing far faster than AI revenue. Wall Street has begun demanding the return — Microsoft trades 30% below its own high on exactly this anxiety. When the patrons' shareholders revolt, orders reprice. [15][8]

ii.

Custom silicon compounds quietly

ASIC shipments growing +45% in 2026 against +16% for merchant GPUs. Anthropic has locked multi-gigawatt TPU supply through 2031; OpenAI committed 6 GW to AMD and co-designs 10 GW with Broadcom. Every gigawatt on an ASIC is one NVIDIA never bids on. [9][10][11]

iii.

Price deflation reached the metal

B200 rental rates fell ≈ 31% in three weeks of June — from $6.11 to $4.22 per hour. Falling utilization economics at the customer precede falling ASPs at the vendor. [14]

iv.

Circular money

GAAP earnings flattered by marks on stakes in NVIDIA's own customers; vendor-financed demand books as revenue today and as risk tomorrow. The quarter's $2.39 GAAP vs $1.87 operating EPS gap is that circularity, printed. [1][3]

v.

The input squeeze

Memory and component inflation is eating the patrons' budgets — USD 25B of Microsoft's 2026 capex is component cost, Meta raised guidance citing the same. Dollars spent on HBM are dollars not spent on accelerators. [7][8]

Exhibit VIII · The China QuestionX

A market carried at zero.

DEC 2025Washington clears the H200 for China — with 25% of every sale remitted to the U.S. Treasury.

JUL 2026Zero units delivered. Beijing steers buyers to Huawei; B30A — the Blackwell China variant — remains denied, as do Blackwell and Rubin proper.
$0
China data-center revenue in guidance

Fifteen months ago this was a USD 4.6B-per-quarter business. Today it is carried at nothing — in the guide, in consensus, and in this examination.

That is the correct way to hold it: a free option, not a dependency. Any H200 delivery is pure upside; further escalation costs the model nothing it hasn't already surrendered.
