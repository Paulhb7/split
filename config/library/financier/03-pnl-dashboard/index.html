Why

Give the founding team one glance-level view of the monthly P&L — where the margin moves, what drives opex, and how much cash the plan consumes — without opening the model.

Context

Brume is a fictional French D2C skincare brand, 18 months post-launch, selling through four channels. Figures are management accounts in EUR thousands, accrual monthly close, Jan 2025 → Jun 2026. Dataset is illustrative.

How to use

Pick a month on the scrubber — or click any trend point, channel bar, or ledger row. Toggle channel chips to isolate channels; every card, chart and the ledger recomputes. Keyboard ← → steps months.

What we assumed

Landed product cost sits in COGS; fulfilment, fees and marketing sit in opex. DTC landed costs improve +2.5 pp from Jul 2025 (renegotiation). Under a channel filter, opex is allocated pro-rata to revenue share — EBITDA then reads as channel contribution. Cash is always company-wide.
