ENGAWAGaudí · Split — a retreat in 3D

縁側 · Engawa

A serene tatami retreat you can move through — the veranda that mediates between the paper room and the raked-gravel garden. Drag to look, glide to a viewpoint, click a surface to hear what it is.

Why
A showcase for Split's library: proof an interior can be delivered as a calm, explorable space, not a flat render. The room breathes when you can walk toward the light instead of imagining it.
Context
One meditative sequence — tatami room · shoji screens · tokonoma alcove · engawa veranda · karesansui garden. Fully procedural geometry & materials; no textures, no images, no build step. Diffuse northern light, deep still shadows.
How to use it
Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Tap a numbered viewpoint below to glide there. Press Walk for WASD movement at eye height. Click any element — tatami, shoji, tokonoma, stone — for a one-line note.
What I assumed
A modest ~4.5 tatami room (traditional 4.5-jō order), north-facing so the light stays soft and even; hinoki post-and-beam, washi screens, one deep-indigo cushion as the only saturated note. Design intent and feel — not stamped construction dimensions.
