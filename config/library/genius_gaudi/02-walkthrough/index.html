About this walk-through ▾

Why

You wanted the space you could move through, not imagine from a paragraph. One postcard render is a shrug; this is the room, breathing, that you orbit and walk.

Context

A compact 7.2 × 5.8 m one-bed — living zone, kitchen nook, a reading corner — with a single Gaudí gesture: a catenary arch (the curve that carries its own load) and a sculpted trencadís shelf, the mosaic pulled from broken tile.

How to use it

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Tap a viewpoint to glide there, or press Walk for WASD + mouse-look at eye height. Click any furniture for its material and role. Toggle Evening to swing the sun warm and low.

What I assumed

A mid-tier finish — natural oak, pale limestone, warm plaster — with teal trencadís as the one accent. This carries look and intent, not measured metric truth or structure: an engineer signs off on what the arch actually carries.
