Casa Trencadís

A single room built entirely in the register of the visionary organic architect — the curve that carries its load, light as a building material, beauty pulled from what was thrown away.
Why
To let you stand inside Gaudí's language rather than read about it — catenary arches, undulating walls, a sculpted vault, and a trencadís mosaic that turns broken tile into a jewel.
Context
One salon, roughly 9 × 7 m, opening south onto a garden through an organic window. Warm Mediterranean daylight. Furnished sparely — a curved bench, a low organic table, a reading nook.
How to use
Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Tap a viewpoint below to glide the camera. Press Walk for first-person (WASD + mouse). Click a feature — the arch, the column, the vault — for the principle at play.
Assumed
Fully procedural geometry & materials (no photos, no textures); a warm ochre / sandstone palette with a teal-gold-rose mosaic accent; a Day / Dusk light you can swing. Intent and look — not a stamped construction plan.
— Gaudí, in Split
