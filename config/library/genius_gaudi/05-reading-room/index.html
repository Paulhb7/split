The Reading Room

A double-height library in the low light of afternoon — walnut, green leather, and aged brass.
About this room ▾

A quiet, scholarly library you can walk through — built as architecture, not a game level.

Why

A showcase for Split's library: prove that a space can be handed over as something you move through, not a flat render. One room, honestly lit, fully explorable.

Context

A double-height study, roughly 7 × 6 m, west-facing: floor-to-ceiling walnut shelving, a mezzanine gallery on a slim stair, a rolling ladder, a leather reading chair, a lamp-lit desk, a tall arched window carrying the last warm hour of daylight.

How to use it

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Tap a viewpoint in the dock below to glide there. Press Walk for first-person WASD + mouse-look (Esc to leave). Click any object — shelves, chair, globe, ladder — for its name, material and size.

What I assumed

A timeless scholar's palette over any single trend: rich walnut, deep forest-green leather, aged brass, cream spines, warm afternoon key. Every surface is procedural — no photos, no textures. This is intent and feel, not a stamped construction drawing.

The straight line belongs to men; the light belongs to the room. — projected once, refined only when the room asked. —G.
