▶About this board

Why

The brief for Marée was wide open on look. Rather than argue palettes in prose, I pinned three finished directions so the boss can point.

Context

iOS app for sea swimmers: tide state, water temp, swell, wind. The home screen carries the decision "in or wait" — so I mocked that screen, not a splash.

How to use it

Read the three cards side by side; the "Now" thumbnail is the same screen each way. Click any card for a larger screen + the reasoning. Verdict at the foot.

What I assumed

Portrait iPhone, single hero metric (water temp), a tide curve, two support stats. Real font pairings, real hex. Copy and numbers are placeholder.

The recommendation

I'd take Estran: sea swimming is mostly a warm, repeated ritual, and its sand-and-algae calm makes the daily "in or wait" read like an invitation rather than a hazard sign, which is where most people live. Sémaphore is right the day the sea turns dangerous — so keep its high-contrast state as Estran's alert mode, and let Abysse become the night theme, rather than choosing one and losing the other two.
