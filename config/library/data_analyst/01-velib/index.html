About this Why it exists, where the data comes from, what was assumed — snapshot of 3 July 2026, 08:01 UTC (10:01 in Paris). ▼

Why

A one-page exploration dashboard of the Vélib’ bike-share network — where the bikes are, which stations are saturated or starved, and how mechanical vs electric splits across the city. Built as a showcase of what the Data Analyst ships from a single open dataset.

Context

Data is a real, unedited snapshot of the dataset velib-disponibilite-en-temps-reel from opendata.paris.fr (Ville de Paris, ODbL), exported 3 July 2026 at ~08:01 UTC — a Friday morning, mid-commute in Paris. 1,517 station records, fully embedded in this file; nothing loads from a server except map tiles and fonts.

How to use

Tap an arrondissement roundel to filter everything — map, KPIs, histograms, top-10 boards. Click any station dot or any row in the top-10 boards to fly to that station. Hover chart bars for exact counts. TOUT resets, GP = suburbs (Grand Paris, 522 stations).

What was assumed

Paris arrondissements are derived from the station-code prefix (the dataset groups all of Paris as one commune) — verified against coordinates. Fill rate = bikes ÷ nominal capacity, clamped at 100%. 875 stations report a usable-dock count that differs from nominal capacity (861 fewer — docks out of service — and 14 more) — nominal capacity is used throughout. 5 stations report capacity 0 and are excluded from fill-rate charts; 20 stations weren’t renting at snapshot time and are drawn grey. Top-10 boards consider stations with ≥ 12 docks. It’s one morning — not a typical day, not a trend.
