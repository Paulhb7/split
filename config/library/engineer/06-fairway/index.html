Why

A showcase piece for Split's library — a small, complete, genuinely playable golf game that proves a self-contained web build can feel like a shipped product.

Context

One file, no build step, no assets: HTML + Canvas2D + vanilla JS. Nine hand-designed holes with a real 2D physics loop (rolling friction, wall bounces, water, sand, slopes, a capture cup).

How to play

Drag back from the ball like a slingshot and release to putt — the arrow shows aim, the meter shows power. Land the ball in the cup in as few strokes as possible; beat par across all nine holes for the best total.

What I assumed

A cheerful flat "sunday course" look (mown grass, blue water, tan sand), pointer-first controls with a keyboard nudge, sound off by default, and reduced-motion honored (particles & shake dialed down).
