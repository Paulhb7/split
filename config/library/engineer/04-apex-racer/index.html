About APEX

Why

A showcase piece for Split's library: proof that a self-contained single-file browser game — a real pseudo-3D arcade racer, no engine, no assets, no build step — can look and feel like something a studio would ship.

Context

Pure Canvas2D using the classic segmented-road projection (the OutRun trick): the road is a list of slices projected into the distance, curves and hills bend and rise as you drive. One HTML file, one Google Font, vanilla JavaScript. Runs at 60fps on desktop and phone.

How to play

Hold gas to build speed, steer into the corners, and weave through traffic. You start with a short clock — barrel through the glowing checkpoint gates to buy more time. Clip a car or an obstacle and you spin out and lose speed, so it stings but never ends the run. Time hits zero, the run ends: your distance is the score. Everything gets faster and busier the further you get.

What I assumed

Bright arcade-cabinet daylight over synthwave neon, keyboard + on-screen touch pads, sound off by default (tap 🔇 to enable), time-attack scoring, and a best-distance saved locally on your machine. Reduced-motion is respected.
