About this artifact+

Why

A flagship landing page for PERIGEE, a fictional orbital-logistics company — rideshare launches plus last-mile orbital transfer vehicles. Built as a library showcase of what the Engineer expert ships.

Context

Single self-contained HTML file, no build step, no stock photography. Hero vehicle, transfer diagram and vehicle sheets are hand-drawn thin-line SVG. All figures are fictional and deliberately conservative.

How to use

Scroll. The T-minus clock and manifest windows compute live from a fixed 16-day launch cadence. The payload estimator gives indicative pricing; the request button pre-fills an email. Anchors in the nav.

What I assumed

Near-black aerospace direction: Saira expanded vs condensed display, blueprint hairlines, one signal-orange accent. Motion is transform-only and honors reduced-motion. Design refs: UI Pro Max (typography, motion-accessibility guidance).

The last mile

Drop-off orbit → your orbit

Rideshare drops you where the bus goes. We take you home.

A shared launch delivers every passenger to one insertion orbit. If your mission needs a different altitude, a different plane, or a precise slot in a constellation, that gap is yours to close — unless APOGEE closes it for you.

  1. 01

    Rideshare drop-off

    PERIGEE-1 inserts the stack at the manifest orbit. APOGEE separates with your spacecraft aboard.

  2. 02

    Phasing & plane change

    A sequence of restartable burns walks altitude, RAAN and phase toward your target — fuel-optimal, ground-supervised.

  3. 03

    Precision insertion

    Release inside the agreed tolerance box, with a state vector you can hand straight to operations.
