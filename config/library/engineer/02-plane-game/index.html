About this

Why

A showcase piece for Split's library: proof that the Engineer can turn "make me a little game" into something genuinely playable and pretty, in one file, in one turn.

Context

A single self-contained HTML document. three.js arrives from a CDN; everything else — the archipelago, the plane, the rings, the sounds — is generated by code when the page opens. No build step, no assets, works by double-clicking the file.

How to use

Press Take flight. Bank with the arrow keys or A/D, climb and dive with W/S, hold Shift to boost. Thread the golden rings; don't touch the mountains or the sea. R restarts, M toggles sound, P pauses.

What you assumed

A desktop browser with a keyboard. Arcade physics over realism — banked turns and generous ring hitboxes. A fixed golden-hour dawn rather than a day cycle. Your best score stays in this browser only (localStorage), nothing leaves the machine.
