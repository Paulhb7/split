Why

A showcase piece for Split's library — a polished, genuinely playable falling-block puzzle that shows a self-contained web build can feel like something a studio would ship.

Context

One HTML file, no build step, no assets — vanilla JavaScript and a 2D canvas, drawn at 60fps. Runs from a double-click or a shared link, on desktop with a keyboard or on a phone with the on-screen pad.

How to play

Shapes fall into the well. Slide and rotate them to fill each row completely; a full row clears and scores. Clear several at once, or clear on consecutive drops, for bonus points. The ghost outline shows where a piece will land, and Hold parks a piece for later. It ends when the stack reaches the top.

What we assumed

Modern falling-block conventions: a 7-bag randomizer (every shape appears once per seven), a next-queue, a hold slot, soft/hard drop, and rotation wall-kicks. Sound is off by default — toggle it on the right. Motion is reduced automatically if your system prefers it.
