The brief

01 · WHY

Why this exists

A single board to see the whole frontier at once and decide where a large enterprise should build, partner, watch or wait. Not a science briefing — a capital-allocation lens on twelve technologies moving at different speeds toward different pay-offs.

02 · CONTEXT

The context

H1 2026 was a threshold season: the first in-vivo CRISPR Phase 3 win, IBM committing to a fault-tolerant machine by 2029, humanoids on live production lines, and agent platforms shipping from OpenAI, Microsoft and AWS. The gap between demo and deployment is now the whole story.

03 · HOW TO USE

How to use it

Read the radar (slide 3): distance from centre = time-to-impact, dot size = capital & attention, colour = theme, amber = act now. Then the ten cards for state-of-art, players, the H1-2026 signal, the enterprise "so what" and the risk. Close on three bets.

04 · WHAT I ASSUMED

What I assumed

Audience: board / strategy committee of a large enterprise, tech-literate, deciding a 3–7yr posture. Scope: frontier tech only (no incumbent SaaS). Period: state of play as of 3 July 2026. Every dated figure is web-sourced; unverifiable claims were dropped.

AI agents & agentic infrastructure

The frontier stopped being a bigger model and became a fleet of agents that plan, use tools and verify each other — with control planes now shipping to manage them.

The signal that changed · H1 2026

OpenAI Frontier launched 5 Feb 2026 — an enterprise control plane for building, deploying and governing agents, interoperable with Google, Microsoft and Anthropic agents. HP, Intuit, Oracle, State Farm, Thermo Fisher and Uber are first adopters; BBVA, Cisco and T-Mobile piloting. Microsoft's Agent 365 and AWS's frontier-agents push make agent fleet-management the new platform battleground.

Enterprise "so what"

The unit of automation shifts from the app to the agent that operates across apps. Winners stand up an agent registry, identity and governance layer now — the 2026 equivalent of the 2010 cloud landing-zone. Buy the control plane; don't hand-roll it.

Legged humanoids crossed from demo reel to live production lines — but the honest global count of robots doing real, paid work is still in the low thousands.

The signal that changed · H1 2026

Tesla reports cumulative Optimus production over 50,000 units and plans external sales in 2026 at a $20–30K target price. The bottleneck moved from "can it walk?" to "can we manufacture the robot?".

Enterprise "so what"

For high-mix warehouse and line work, this is a 3–5yr capex conversation, not a moonshot — but the near-term ROI is in narrow, repetitive stations, not general labour. Pilot in a bounded cell; underwrite on cycle-time, not on the highlight reel.

Quantum computing

2026 is the year the field committed to a dated path to fault tolerance — the overhead per logical qubit fell an order of magnitude, and a credited-partner Level-2 machine went public.

03
2029
IBM Starling target: 200 logical qubits · 100M gates, built in Poughkeepsie (IBM)
~1:100
physical→logical overhead via qLDPC codes, down from ~1:1,000 (IBM/Google, May 2026)
13,000×
Google "Quantum Echoes" speed-up vs classical — first verifiable advantage (Willow)
The signal that changed · H1 2026

IBM published a concrete roadmap to a large-scale fault-tolerant machine by 2029 (Starling), using the qLDPC "bicycle" architecture — 12 logical qubits from 144 physical. It also hit a 10× QEC-decoding speed-up a year early. Quantinuum's H2 became the first system to reach Microsoft's Level-2 "Resilient" phase — error rates ~800× below physical.

Enterprise "so what"

No cryptographically-relevant machine yet — but the roadmaps are now dated. The only no-regret 2026 move is post-quantum cryptography migration ("harvest-now, decrypt-later" is a live threat) and quantum literacy in materials/chemistry R&D functions.

Key players & labs

GoogleWillow · Quantum Echoes

MicrosoftAzure Quantum · resilience tiers
IonQ · Riverlaneion traps · QEC decoding stack
The risk

Timeline slippage is the base rate; "logical qubit" counts are not comparable across vendors. Fault-tolerant advantage on a useful problem is still years out — treat 2029 dates as engineering targets, not delivery guarantees.

Gene editing & longevity

The field crossed its most important line yet: an in-body CRISPR edit succeeded in a pivotal Phase 3 — moving gene editing from ex-vivo procedure to potential one-shot medicine.

The signal that changed · H1 2026

Intellia's HAELO trial is the world's first positive pivotal Phase 3 for an in-vivo CRISPR therapy (published NEJM, presented at EAACI 2026). In parallel, Casgevy's label expanded to ages 2+ for sickle-cell and beta-thalassemia. Base and prime editing are entering Phase 1 with cleaner safety profiles.

Enterprise "so what"

For pharma, payers and health systems: in-vivo editing changes the economics of chronic disease — a one-time edit vs lifelong therapy. The value question moves from efficacy to manufacturing, delivery (LNP/AAV) and reimbursement design.

Near-term value is clinical — restoring communication and control for paralysis/ALS — and it is a medtech/regulatory play, not a consumer product. Watch Synchron's PMA path as the field's first real market signal; consumer neural I/O remains a decade+ out.

For most enterprises this is a watch-and-optionality item, not a 5-yr supply plan. The near-term signal that matters is hyperscaler power procurement (Microsoft–Helion): fusion is being pulled by AI's energy demand, so track it via data-centre PPAs.

Advanced materials & superconductors

AI is compressing materials discovery from years to weeks — and it broke a 30-year record — but the prize, ambient room-temperature superconductivity, remains unvalidated.

10

~325K

>100GPa

The signal that changed · H1 2026

AI-guided design produced "Grokene", a graphene-derived superlattice with a computed critical temperature near room temperature — though its 2D nature limits the observed transition to ~120K in monolayers, and it is not yet experimentally validated. A separate group broke a 30-year ambient-pressure record. AI materials pipelines (post-GNoME) are the real, bankable shift.

Enterprise "so what"

Don't bet on room-temperature superconductivity — bet on AI-accelerated materials discovery as a capability. For chemicals, batteries, aerospace and semis, an in-house or partnered ML materials pipeline is becoming a durable R&D advantage.

SYNTHESIS13 / 16
The three-speed frontier

Not one frontier — three, moving at different speeds.

SPEED 1 · DEPLOY NOW

Real, at scale, today

AI agents · humanoids on lines · AI smart-glasses. Capital is enormous, products ship, ROI is being underwritten. The risk here is governance and over-buying, not whether it works.

SPEED 2 · BUILD OPTIONALITY

Pivotal proof in hand

In-vivo gene editing · synthetic bio · solid-state cells · minimally-invasive BCI. The science crossed a line in H1 2026; the constraint is now manufacturing, delivery & reimbursement. Position, partner, pilot.

SPEED 3 · WATCH & SEED

Roadmap, not delivery

Quantum · fusion · implanted BCI · RT superconductors. Dated targets exist (IBM 2029, SPARC Q>1 2027) but delivery is years out. The right posture is no-regret prep & small options.

The strategy error is treating all three as one bucket — either dismissing the frontier as "years away" (missing Speed 1) or chasing every headline (over-investing in Speed 3). Sort by speed first, then allocate.

THE RECOMMENDATION14 / 16
Where to place the bets

Three bets for the board

BET 01 · ACT NOW

Buy the agent control plane, not another model

Agentic infrastructure is the one frontier that is live, funded and compounding. Stand up an agent registry, identity and governance layer in 2026 — before shadow-agents proliferate. Treat OpenAI Frontier / Microsoft Agent 365 as the landing-zone decision of the decade.
Signal: $2.59T AI spend '26 · 40% of apps agent-enabled by year-end
BET 02 · POSITION

Take options on the proofs of H1 2026

In-vivo CRISPR, synthetic bio and solid-state crossed pivotal thresholds. Don't build them — partner, co-invest and secure supply. The value has moved from "does it work?" to manufacturing, delivery and reimbursement. First movers on supply agreements win the 2028 curve.
Signal: 87% in-vivo CRISPR P3 · QSE-5 at 844 Wh/L · biofoundries autonomous
BET 03 · HEDGE

Make the no-regret moves on the long horizon

For quantum and fusion, delivery is 2029+. The disciplined moves are cheap and mandatory: start post-quantum cryptography migration now ("harvest-now, decrypt-later" is real), and track fusion via hyperscaler power deals, not press releases. Seed literacy; don't over-commit capital.
Signal: IBM Starling 2029 · Helion→Microsoft 2028 (aggressive) · PQC is urgent
THE ONE LINE

Deploy the agents, take options on the biology, and buy insurance on the physics. Sort by speed, allocate to signal.
