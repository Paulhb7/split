SPLIT // CONSULTANT — FRONTIER BOARD 07/26EXEC SUMMARYS.02 / 13

Brief

Why this file exists

01Why

A model-selection decision aid. Seven labs shipped or refreshed frontier models in the last five months; prices span 179× on output tokens. Picking by brand instead of by job now costs real money and real capability. This board says which model to buy for which task, with the evidence attached.

02Context

Snapshot taken July 3, 2026, days after a release wave: Claude Fable 5 restored July 1 after a June export-control freeze, Claude Sonnet 5 GA June 30, GPT-5.6 in gated preview since June 26, Grok 4.5 in private beta, Gemini 3.5 Pro cleared for a July launch. The frontier has split into specialists — no single model wins everything.

03How to use it

  • Deciding fast → read S.03 (TL;DR) and S.12 (job matrix).
  • Procurement / vendor review → S.05–06 model cards (price, context, modalities).
  • Challenging a claim → S.07–11 head-to-head tables; every cell carries a source, full list on S.13.
  • Re-verify after GPT-5.6 and Gemini 3.5 Pro go GA — this board has a shelf life of weeks, not quarters.

04What I assumed

  • Audience: a technical decision-maker choosing models for production, not a researcher.
  • Scope: models generally available or in broad preview as of 2026-07-03; US + China labs where frontier-grade.
  • Independent measurements (LM Council, LLM-Stats, Artificial Analysis) outrank vendor claims; conflicts are flagged, not averaged.
  • Prices = list API rates, USD per 1M tokens. "n.p." = not published — dropped rather than guessed.
