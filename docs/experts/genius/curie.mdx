<Info>**Genius** · researcher · slug `genius_curie`</Info>

You bring a disease name, a gene, or a clinical description. Curie turns it into
a real, structured scientific investigation you could not run alone — disease →
causal protein → 3D structure → druggable pocket → **repurposing hypothesis**,
anchored at every step to public biomedical databases and current literature.

Where [Scientist](/experts/super/scientist) summarizes the literature, Curie
**runs the computational pipeline only a specialist could assemble**.

## What it delivers

One self-contained **`report.html`** — the whole investigation on a single page:

- the disease grounded (prevalence, inheritance, phenotype) and its causal gene(s),
- the **AlphaFold structure embedded and spinning** (3Dmol.js, structure inline),
- the evidence base + the **clinical-trial landscape** (ongoing / completed / terminated),
- similar mechanisms across diseases (PrimeKG graph reasoning),
- a **repurposing table**: already-approved drugs that hit the shared mechanism,
  ranked by feasibility, cross-referenced to ClinicalTrials.gov,
- an honest limits section + the wet-lab assays that would confirm each lead.

## When to pick it

Pick **Curie** for rare/orphan diseases, target identification, structural
biology, and drug repurposing. Pick [Scientist](/experts/super/scientist) instead
when you want a literature review or state-of-the-art map without the
target→structure→docking pipeline.

## How to drive it

Start from a disease, a gene, or a clinical question:

```
Investigate a rare disease
Find the target protein for this condition
Propose a drug to repurpose for this disease
```

Attach a clinical summary, a gene list, or a paper if you have one — it's folded
into the investigation's `inputs/`.

## What comes back

The `report.html` renders **live in the artifact pane** (WebGL viewer, so it's
treated as interactive — open it in a browser to print). Everything is saved to
`~/Desktop/curie-<disease>-<date>/`: the report at the root, raw `.pdb`/`.cif`
and data JSON under `outputs/`, and your inputs under `inputs/`. Iterations land
in a `v2/` subfolder — v1 is never overwritten.

## Leans on

A real library-backed scientific stack — see [Skills](/concepts/skills):

| Skill | Role |
|---|---|
| `database-lookup` | 78 biomedical/clinical DBs (OMIM, Open Targets, ClinVar, UniProt, AlphaFold, ChEMBL, DrugBank, ClinicalTrials.gov…) |
| `primekg` | disease ↔ gene ↔ drug ↔ phenotype graph — cross-disease repurposing reasoning |
| `paper-lookup` / `paper-search` | 10 literature databases (PubMed, PMC, bioRxiv, Semantic Scholar…) |
| `biopython` | parse/analyse sequences & structures, variant mapping |
| `diffdock` | protein–ligand docking — the binding-pocket / repurposing screen |
| `rdkit` / `medchem` | cheminformatics + medicinal-chemistry triage |
| `esm` | ESMFold de-novo folding when AlphaFold DB has no entry |
| `molecular-dynamics` | OpenMM/MDAnalysis — refine a pose, short MD |
| `scanpy` / `pymatgen` | single-cell expression · materials (off-path) |

## Prerequisites & limits

- **Runs anywhere:** the database + literature skills are pure REST/HTTP. Some
  raise their rate limits with an optional key (`NCBI_API_KEY`, etc.).
- **Needs compute:** the heavy skills (`esm` folding, `diffdock`, `molecular-dynamics`)
  need their Python deps and ideally a GPU. Without them, Curie leans on
  **AlphaFold DB retrieval + docking analysis** rather than de-novo compute —
  and says which path it took.
- **Honesty by design:** a predicted structure is not an experimental one; a
  repurposing hypothesis from pocket similarity is a *lead*, not a treatment.
  Curie states what would have to be true experimentally for each claim to hold.
