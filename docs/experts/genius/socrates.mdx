<Info>**Genius** · recruiter · slug `genius_socrates`</Info>

You bring a task you keep doing yourself and wish someone else owned. Socrates is
the meta-expert whose only job is to design *other* experts: it interviews you,
proposes a persona, and on approval adds a new expert to your personal roster.

It doesn't research, write decks, or drive apps — it asks one pointed question at
a time until your need is sharp, then drafts.

## What it delivers

A **new custom expert** added to your roster — written as two files under your
private data directory (never the shipped catalogue):

- a **persona file** (`<VAR_DIR>/experts/<slug>.md`) — the mission paragraph,
  tone line, and a "How you work" section (60–120 lines),
- a **metadata entry** (`<VAR_DIR>/experts/<slug>.json`) — `label`, `role`,
  `kind`, `blurb`, and `skills` (always `[]` in v1).

The result is a persona you can mention as `@<Label>` in a fresh session.

## When to pick it

Pick **Socrates** when no expert on your roster fits a recurring job and you want
to create one — or when you're not sure what's missing and need to be interviewed
to find out. Pick any other genius (e.g. [Curie](/experts/genius/curie) for
science, [Brontë](/experts/genius/bronte) for fiction) when the expert you need
already exists — Socrates builds the roster, it doesn't do the work.

## How to drive it

Start the interview:

```
Help me create a custom expert
Which expert is missing from my roster?
Interview me to pin down my need
```

Nothing to attach — it works by conversation. Expect it to move through five
beats: **pain** (the specific job), **shape** (audience, frequency, format,
tone), **name** (a genius-of-the-domain label), **skeleton** (the persona drafted
inline), and **commit** (the files written on your "go").

## What comes back

A short, plain-spoken interview in chat, then the drafted persona and JSON shown
verbatim for approval. On *"go"* it writes the two files and confirms with their
**absolute paths** and a one-line *"Start a new session and mention @<Label>."*
The new expert then appears in your roster. Nothing is written silently and
nothing touches the shipped `config/` directory, so a Split update never
overwrites your custom experts.

## Leans on

No skills — this is a **built-in interview-and-draft flow**, not a tool. It uses
the Socratic method: short, pointed questions (one per turn, no menus, no filler)
to surface what you actually need, proposes a "genius of the domain" name, drafts
the persona structure inline, iterates with you, and on approval writes the
persona + metadata files to your private `<VAR_DIR>/experts/`. It checks the new
`<slug>` against existing experts and suffixes `_2`, `_3` on collision.

## Prerequisites & limits

- **No keys or accounts** — it's pure conversation plus two local file writes.
- **Personas are text-only in v1** — no avatar, no emoji.
- **No skills assigned.** Recruiter v1 always writes `"skills": []`; you can add
  skills later by editing the created JSON entry.
- **One interview = one expert.** For a second, finish the first and start over.
- **It designs, it doesn't run.** The new expert is a persona only — its actual
  work happens when you start a session and mention it.
