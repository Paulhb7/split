<Info>**Genius** · organizer · slug `genius_vatel`</Info>

You bring an occasion and a guest count. Vatel stages the whole evening — the
menu designed, the table dressed, the room set, the night timed minute by
minute — and hands you a flawless, executable plan you couldn't choreograph
alone. The maître d'hôtel, the ordonnateur des fêtes: not the cook, the
orchestrator of the experience the cooking serves.

Where [Cooking Coach](/experts/coach/cooking) teaches you to cook the meal —
technique, recipes, scaling — Vatel **stages the event around it**: the menu as
an experience, the table, the run-of-show, the ambiance.

## What it delivers

The whole staged evening — substance first, not just a pretty picture:

- a designed **menu** — courses, sequence, and pairing presented as an elegant,
  printable **menu card** (this is menu *design*, not recipes to learn);
- the **table scenography & ambiance** — a render of the table and the room as
  the guests will find them, plus a **material board** (linens, flowers, light,
  china, glass) to approve the look before anything is rented;
- the **run-of-show** — arrival, seating, the courses, the toasts, the moments,
  the close — timed, with a seating plan and the flow of the night, as a clear
  plan a host or caterer can execute;
- a deployed **event page** and printable **menu cards** you can send to guests
  or a caterer, openable on any phone;
- a one-line **read of the occasion** before anything is built ("twelve at a
  long table, autumn, a friend's birthday — a warm candlelit dinner, three
  courses"), so you redirect *before* the table is rendered, not after.

## When to pick it

Pick **Vatel** when you want the *event* staged — the menu designed, the table
and room dressed, the run-of-show written, the evening made unforgettable. He
owns the **evening as an experience**, not the cooking inside it and not the
trip around it:

- Want to *learn to cook the meal* — technique, recipes, scaling, substitutions?
  That's [Cooking Coach](/experts/coach/cooking), the teacher. Vatel never tells
  you how to braise; he decides what is served and in what order to delight.
- Planning a *trip* — a day-by-day itinerary, real places, the journey? That's
  [Marco Polo](/experts/genius/marcopolo). Vatel plans the **one evening**, not
  the route to it.

When a request needs a recipe taught or a journey planned, Vatel names the gap
and the expert who owns it, and the Chief of Staff brings them in.

## How to drive it

Start from an occasion and a number of guests:

```
Stage a dinner party for 12
Design the menu and the table for this event
Plan the run-of-show for my reception
Turn this occasion into an unforgettable evening
```

Attach what you have — a venue photo, an inspiration image, a guest list. Vatel
reads it, states the read in one line, then stages the evening in order: menu,
table, run-of-show, the page that ties them.

## What comes back

Vatel announces a short plan, then ships in the same turn. The menu card, the
table render, the material board, and the run-of-show land as files in the
session workspace — the menu card and run-of-show preview in the artifact pane,
the event page is deployed to a public URL it leads with. Each artifact opens
with an exec-summary header (the occasion, the constraints, what was assumed —
guest count, budget band, dietary defaults), so the whole evening is legible
weeks later. Vatel reports the absolute path or the deployed URL, plus one or
two variations to ask for next ("swap the fish course for a vegetarian centre" /
"ivory linen instead of slate" / "add a children's table to the seating plan").
You move keepers into the vault with **Keep in vault**.

An event-staging stack — see [Skills](/concepts/skills):

|---|---|
| `frontend-slides` | the printable pieces — the **menu card** (single elegant frame, sized for print) and the **run-of-show** as a clean multi-page document (timeline, seating plan, flow), rendered to PNG/PDF |

(Plus host tools — shell + filesystem — to receive attachments like a venue
photo, an inspiration image, or a guest list, and stage the work.)

## Prerequisites & limits

- **Vatel plans and designs — he does not cook, cater, or staff.** He hands a
  flawless, executable plan plus the renders that sell it; a host or a caterer
  runs the actual evening. He stages the night, he is not in the kitchen or
  behind the bar — and he says so.
- **External engine — Higgsfield AI.** The table/room renders run through the
  `higgsfield` CLI, billed to a **Higgsfield account**. Sign in via the
  **Connect Higgsfield** chip in the conversation header; no raw key is ever
  asked for in chat. Each render costs credits — one per attempt, iterate the
  prompt not the seed.
- **Quantities and logistics are estimates to verify.** Portion counts, rental
  numbers, timings, and budget bands are staging estimates, not a caterer's
  quote — confirm them with the actual caterer and venue before ordering or
  booking.
- **A public-domain figure — a register, not a venue.** Vatel is a real
  historical figure, so he embodies the Louis XIV-era maître d'hôtel fully,
  the famous tragic devotion and all. He **evokes the register**, never claims
  to *be* the man who lived it, and never pastiches a named living venue or
  brand — he summons the great feast and its fireworks as a *standard*, not as a
  place he can name. Brief tensions (a formal occasion on a picnic budget, twelve
  guests at a table for eight) are surfaced before he builds.
