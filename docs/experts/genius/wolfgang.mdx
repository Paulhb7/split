<Info>**Genius** · creator · slug `genius_wolfgang`</Info>

You bring a feeling, a moment, a picture, or a one-line brief. Wolfgang turns it
into a finished, mixed track you could not prompt for yourself — cross-modal
translation from image, poem, or video into the score it implies.

Where [Homer](/experts/genius/homer) produces spoken audio (podcasts, voices),
Wolfgang **composes the music itself** — songs, scores, ambient beds.

## What it delivers

A single **mixed audio file** — one finished piece, ready to drop into a project:

- **songs, scores, soundtracks, audio logos, ambient beds** — 4 seconds to a few
  minutes, with sung or spoken voice when the form asks for it,
- **cross-modal translation**: an image (painting, photo, album cover) → the score
  it implies; a poem → music for the words; a short video → its score, paced to
  the cuts; a vague brief → a sharpened concept you can react to,
- a one-line **read of the input** before generation ("you gave me a Rothko at
  dusk; I hear cello drone + slow piano"), so you redirect *before* the audio is
  made, not after,
- one or two concrete next variations on delivery ("same track, +20bpm" / "no
  vocals, strings only" / "sax replaces piano").

## When to pick it

Pick **Wolfgang** when you want original music — a theme, a song, a soundtrack,
a bed. Pick [Homer](/experts/genius/homer) instead when the output is spoken
audio (a podcast episode, narration, an audiobook), or
[Kubrick](/experts/genius/kubrick) when you want a complete short film where the
music is one layer among visuals and voice.

## How to drive it

Start from a feeling, a brief, or an attachment:

```
Compose a theme for this project
Write a song about this topic
Make an ambient soundtrack
```

Attach the source it should translate — an image, a poem, a video, or a written
brief. Wolfgang reads it, states its read in one line, then writes the prompt.

## What comes back

A single mixed track (Sonilo returns a finished mix, **not separated stems**).
Wolfgang reports the **absolute file path** so you can play or share it, plus one
or two variations to ask for next. The Higgsfield job prints the **result media
URL** for the generated audio; the file lands in the session workspace, and you
move keepers into the vault with **Keep in vault**.

## Leans on

One generation engine, two audio paths — see [Skills](/concepts/skills):

| Skill | Role |
|---|---|
| `higgsfield-generate` | the composition engine: **Sonilo Music** (`sonilo_music`, `--prompt` + `--duration`) for songs / scores / beds / audio logos; **Mirelo Text to Audio** for non-musical SFX, foley, ambience |

(Plus host tools — shell + filesystem — to receive attachments, inspect them,
and stage the prompt.)

## Prerequisites & limits

- **External engine — Higgsfield AI.** Generation runs through the `higgsfield`
  CLI, not an in-house engine. Wolfgang specifically drives Higgsfield's
  **music/audio models** (Sonilo Music, Mirelo Text to Audio). It needs a
  **Higgsfield account** and `higgsfield auth login`; in the app, sign in via the
  **Connect Higgsfield** chip in the conversation header. No raw key is ever
  asked for in chat.
- **Costs credits, and some models need a paid plan.** Each track is a real
  Higgsfield generation that costs credits; certain models require a **paid
  Higgsfield plan**. Wolfgang is deliberate — one track per attempt, iterate the
  *prompt* not the seed.
- **No separated stems.** Sonilo returns a finished mix, not isolated
  vocals/piano/drums. If you need stems, that isn't available from this engine —
  Wolfgang offers prompt variations instead.
- **No pastiche of living artists** (legal exposure). It matches a tradition, a
  movement, or a register without naming the artist. Contradictory briefs (soft
  poem, "aggressive industrial") are surfaced *before* generating so you pick the
  side.
