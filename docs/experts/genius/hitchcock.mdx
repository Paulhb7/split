<Info>**Genius** · creator · slug `genius_hitchcock`</Info>

You bring an idea — a teaser, an ad, a music video, a Ken-Burns clip from a
photo. Hitchcock directs it into a finished short you can share: visuals, score,
voice, and the cut. The master of suspense, who storyboards the whole film
before a frame is shot — so **you see the board and approve it before any
expensive video is generated.**

Where [Wolfgang](/experts/genius/wolfgang) composes the music and
[Homer](/experts/genius/homer) produces the spoken audio, Hitchcock **directs the
whole short** and folds those layers into a single edited film.

## The board comes first

Video is the expensive, error-prone step, so Hitchcock finishes the film on paper
before he shoots it:

1. **Turn 1 — the board.** The fil rouge (logline + shot list + style sheet)
   *and* one cheap still per shot (Z Image / GPT Image 2). A real v1 you can see,
   not a question.
2. **You react** — fix a shot, drop a frame, change the character — and Hitchcock
   iterates the *stills* (near-free) until the board holds.
3. **Turn 2 — the film.** Only once you approve does he shoot the video (Seedance
   2.0, image-to-video from the approved frames). No video credits spent on an
   unapproved frame.

## What it delivers

A **finished short film — 15–30 seconds, exceptionally 60** — preceded by an
approved board and delivered as a re-editable folder:

- the **board**: a **logline**, a **shot list** (3–6 shots), a **style sheet**,
  and one **storyboard still per shot** — shipped and approved before the shoot,
- the master cut as **`master-h.mp4`** (1920×1080) and a **`master-v.mp4`**
  (1080×1920) vertical variant when the brief asks for both (social ads, TikTok /
  Instagram),
- the **raw shots** (`shots/01.mp4 … 06.mp4`), the **score** (`score.mp3`), the
  **voice tracks** (`voice/*.mp3`), the **board stills** (`board/`), the **shot
  list** (`shot-list.md`), the **style sheet** (`style.md`), and the **`ffmpeg`
  recipe** (`cut.sh`) — so you or a human editor can re-cut without re-generating,
- a recurring character held consistent shot to shot via a trained **Soul
  Character** `reference_id`.

## When to pick it

Pick **Hitchcock** when you want a complete short film — visuals, score, and
voice cut together, with the cheap board approved before the dear shoot. Pick
[Wolfgang](/experts/genius/wolfgang) instead when you only need the *music*, or
[Homer](/experts/genius/homer) when you only need *spoken audio*. Hitchcock will
say no to "make me a 5-minute film" and counter-propose a stronger short —
multi-shot coherence in generative video is hard, and a strong 20-second clip
beats a flat two-minute one.

## How to drive it

Start from an idea, a scene, or a brief:

```
Direct a short film from this idea
Storyboard this scene
Compose the image, music, and voice
```

Attach a reference photo, a sketch, or a written brief — Hitchcock opens with the
board (logline + shot list + storyboard stills) so you redirect **before** any
video is generated.

## What comes back

First the **board** (frames + fil rouge) for your nod; then, after approval, a
re-editable **folder** with the master cut(s) at the root and every layer beside
them (raw shots, score, voice, board stills, shot list, style sheet, `cut.sh`).
Hitchcock reports the **path** plus one line on what to refine next ("shot 3 loses
the character — re-shoot it image-to-video from shot 2's last frame, with the Soul
`reference_id`"). Each Higgsfield job prints its **result media URL**. Files land
in the session workspace; move keepers into the vault with **Keep in vault**.

## Prerequisites & limits

- **External engine — Higgsfield AI.** Generation runs through the `higgsfield`
  CLI, not an in-house engine. Hitchcock drives Higgsfield's **image models** for
  the board (Z Image / GPT Image 2), its **video models** (Seedance 2.0, Kling
  3.0, Cinema Studio) for the shoot, and its **Soul ID** character models, plus
  Sonilo/Mirelo/Inworld for score, ambience, and voice. It needs a **Higgsfield
  account** and `higgsfield auth login`; in the app, sign in via the **Connect
  Higgsfield** chip in the conversation header. No raw key is ever asked for in
  chat.
- **Costs credits, and video / Soul training need a paid plan.** Each shot is real
  money — video is the expensive part, **a free plan won't cover it** (board
  stills on Z Image are near-free), and **Soul Character training requires a paid
  plan**. The board-first order is exactly how Hitchcock keeps the video credits
  from being wasted: nothing is shot until the frame is approved.
- **Multi-shot coherence is still hard.** A trained Soul Character holds the face
  far better than re-prompting, but lighting and framing can still jump —
  Hitchcock catches the drift, surfaces it, and you decide whether to re-shoot or
  live with it. It pushes back on scope creep in favour of the short that holds.
