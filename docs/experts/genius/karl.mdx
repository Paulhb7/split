<Info>**Genius** · creator · slug `genius_karl`</Info>

You bring a mood, a fabric, a word, or a bad phone photo of a shoe. Karl — a
couturier — gives back a finished thing you could not draw yourself: the garment
designed, worn on a model, shot as a lookbook. Imperious, tri-lingual, never
satisfied, tiresomely right.

Where [DaVinci](/experts/genius/davinci) builds visual *worlds* and brand
identities, Karl makes **clothes and shoes** — and shoots the **lookbook**.

## What it delivers

A collection, taken from sketch to lookbook:

- **the piece designed** — a garment or a pair of shoes from a mood / fabric /
  sketch / reference, with the silhouette, line, fabric story and palette stated,
  then *rendered* (not described),
- **the piece worn** — a lookbook frame / editorial / virtual try-on on a single
  **house model** (one trained face) so the collection reads as a body of work,
- **the campaign image** — the hero still: the look composed, lit and styled like
  a campaign or editorial spread, not a catalogue snap,
- **the lookbook** — one page (line sheet + every look on the house model)
  deployed to a URL the boss forwards,
- **a verdict** — an honest, cutting read of the brief before a credit is spent,
  and one or two variations to ask next ("same coat, belted" / "same shoe,
  oxblood").

## When to pick it

Pick **Karl** when the deliverable is **fashion** — a garment, a shoe, a capsule
collection, a lookbook, a campaign image. Pick [DaVinci](/experts/genius/davinci) instead
for a brand identity, a 3D scene, or product UI; [Designer](/experts/super/designer)
for app/site mockups; the [Influencer](/experts/super/influencer) when the goal is
selling an existing product with viral ads rather than designing a new one.

## How to drive it

Start from a mood, a fabric, or an attachment:

```
Design a capsule collection from this mood
Put this piece on a model
Build a lookbook for these looks
Turn this sketch into a finished garment
```

Attach what anchors taste — a fabric photo, a sketch, a reference image. Karl
states his read in one cutting line, then renders three directions as v1. If the
brief is genuinely too thin to cut from — no mood, no fabric, no point of view —
he throws it back **once** and makes you defend or sharpen it ("justify it,
Liebling"), then ships.

## What comes back

Rendered looks, campaign stills, and a lookbook page. Karl reports the **absolute
file path** (or the deployed URL) plus variations to ask for next. The Higgsfield
jobs print the **result media URLs**; files land in the session workspace, and you
move keepers into the vault with **Keep in vault**.

## Leans on

A visual atelier on one generation engine — see [Skills](/concepts/skills):

| Skill | Role |
|---|---|
| `higgsfield-generate` | the atelier: garment/footwear concept images and campaign stills (GPT Image 2 / Nano Banana / Soul 2.0). **Stills only — no video** (the runway clip is out; it burns credits for little) |
| `higgsfield-soul-id` | train the **house model's** face once → a `reference_id` reused so the same model wears every look across the lookbook (the weapon against face drift) |
| `higgsfield-product-photoshoot` | the piece **on a model** — `virtual_model_tryout`, `lifestyle_scene`, `moodboard_pin`, `restyle`; backend assembles the prompt |
| `taste-design` | the collection's `DESIGN.md` (palette, silhouette language, fabric story, references, anti-patterns), audited before delivery |
| `frontend-slides` | the lookbook / line sheet / fashion plates rendered as pages, exported to PNG/PDF |
| `web-deploy` | ship the lookbook page to a public URL |

(Plus host tools — shell + filesystem — to receive a fabric photo / sketch /
reference and stage the prompt.)

## Prerequisites & limits

- **External engine — Higgsfield AI.** Concept images and campaign stills run
  through the `higgsfield` CLI; it needs a **Higgsfield account** and `higgsfield
  auth login`. In the app, sign in via the **Connect Higgsfield** chip in the
  conversation header — no raw key is ever asked for in chat.
- **Costs credits — stills only, no video.** Each render is a real Higgsfield
  generation; the animated runway clip is deliberately out (it burned credits for
  little). Karl is deliberate — one look per attempt, iterate the *prompt* not the
  seed.
- **The model drifts without Soul ID.** Cast the house model first; even then
  lighting/framing can jump between shots — Karl catches it and re-shoots the one
  frame rather than pretending it's fine.
- **No pastiche of a living house or designer by name** (legal exposure). Karl
  matches a tradition, a movement, a register — never the trademark. Ask for
  "Chanel" and you get the line that house taught the world, without the name.
