<Info>**Super** · creator · slug `influencer`</Info>

You bring a product (a photo, a URL, a line) and a goal (sell / launch / build
awareness). Influencer turns it into social content that *travels* — hooks, UGC
videos, ad packs, and the recurring AI face that fronts the brand — with an honest
read on whether it'll perform.

A growth marketer who knows the first second is the whole game: hook-first,
platform-native, scored for retention.

## What it delivers

Post-ready creative, in packs, not singles:

- **the hook** — several angles for the first ~1.5 seconds that decide everything,
  with the strongest one named and built first,
- **UGC + ad video** — presenter / talking-head, unboxing, how-to, product demo,
  TV-spot (Marketing Studio), optionally fronted by a **consistent AI avatar**,
- **static social** — ad creative packs, carousels, hero banners, Pinterest pins,
  multi-format (feed / story / 9:16),
- **the virality read** — a finished video scored for hook strength, attention and
  retention, with the single fix that would raise it (Virality Predictor),
- **the drop** — a campaign: a week of content, a calendar, a link-in-bio /
  landing page.

## When to pick it

Pick **Influencer** when the goal is **selling or launching** an existing product
with content that performs. Pick [Karl](/experts/genius/karl) instead to *design*
the product (garments, the runway); [DaVinci](/experts/genius/davinci) to build the
brand *identity*; [Hitchcock](/experts/genius/hitchcock) for a short film as art rather
than an ad that converts.

## How to drive it

Start from a product and a goal:

```
Make a viral hook for this product
Spin up a week of UGC ads
Score this ad's virality
Launch a campaign for this drop
```

Attach a product photo or a product URL. Influencer announces a plan (platform /
format / hook angle / how many), then ships a *pack* of variants in the same turn —
social is a numbers game, so one creative is a coin flip.

## What comes back

The creative URLs / paths + the **virality read** + one concrete next move ("hook B
scored higher — want three more in that lane?"). The Higgsfield jobs print the
**result media URLs**; files land in the session workspace, and you move keepers
into the vault with **Keep in vault**.

## Prerequisites & limits

- **External engine — Higgsfield AI.** Generation runs through the `higgsfield`
  CLI; it needs a **Higgsfield account** and `higgsfield auth login`. In the app,
  sign in via the **Connect Higgsfield** chip in the conversation header — no raw
  key is ever asked for in chat.
- **Costs credits — video is the expensive part.** Marketing Studio video won't
  run on a free plan; one generation per attempt, iterate the prompt.
- **The virality score is a proxy, not a promise.** A high score means stronger
  stimulus and attention, not guaranteed reach — it's a creative-testing signal
  ("this hook beats that one"), reported honestly.
- **No deceptive claims, no stolen likeness.** No fabricated testimonials, fake
  before/afters, or health/income claims it can't back; the avatar is a *generated*
  face, never a real creator's likeness lifted without consent.
