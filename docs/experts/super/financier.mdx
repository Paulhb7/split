<Info>**Super** · analyst · slug `financier`</Info>

You bring a ticker, a portfolio, a model to check, or a budget to project.
Financier pulls live numbers, leads with the call, and ships the file — a
stock pitch deck, an Excel model, a live dashboard, or an advisory memo,
every number carrying a source and an as-of date.

## What it delivers

It wears four hats — markets & investing, corporate finance / FP&A, personal
& wealth, accounting & reporting — and ships the matching artifact:

- **Stock Pitch** — an 11-slide HTML deck (preset `split-default`, recolored
  to the company's brand): cover with the BUY/HOLD/SELL call, snapshot KPIs,
  thesis, 3-year financials, valuation (comps + DCF range), catalysts, macro
  & sector environment, risks, the recommendation,
- **Portfolio Review** — an 8-slide deck **plus** a live HTML `dashboard`
  monitor (KPI tiles, equity curve vs benchmark, sector donut, positions
  table, macro tape),
- **Valuation / FP&A** — an **`.xlsx` model** on the Cover → Assumptions →
  Data → Model → Scenarios convention,
- **Model Audit** — a 🔴🟡🟢 findings report (+ optional annotated workbook)
  on any `.xlsx` — mechanical defects from the scanner, financial-logic layer
  on top (balance ties, 3-statement linkage, sign/unit consistency),
- **Personal & wealth** — a **`.docx`** memo (FR tax default), optionally an
  Excel projection.

Every deliverable carries an **Assumptions** slide/sheet — the override hook.

## When to pick it

Pick **Financier** for anything money: valuing a security or business,
analyzing a market and its comparables, building or auditing a 3-statement
model, modelling a budget/runway, or a P&L tracking dashboard. Pick
[Consultant](/experts/super/consultant) instead when the question is
market/competitive strategy rather than the numbers themselves, or
[Data Analyst](/experts/super/data_analyst) for a general CSV/Excel
exploration that isn't financial.

## How to drive it

Name the workflow and the inputs:

```
Build a P&L tracking dashboard
Analyze this market and its comparables
Model an 18-month budget
```

Attach a holdings CSV (`TICKER,SHARES,COST_BASIS,CURRENCY`) for a portfolio,
or an `.xlsx` to audit. For a portfolio, Financier asks for the benchmark and
base currency if there's any ambiguity.

## What comes back

Decks and dashboards render **live in the artifact pane**. Files land in
user-visible folders: slides at `~/Desktop/<ticker-or-portfolio>-<YYYYMMDD>.html`,
Excel at `~/Desktop/<topic>-<YYYYMMDD>.xlsx`, memos at
`~/Desktop/<topic>-<YYYYMMDD>.docx`, and a portfolio dashboard at
`~/Documents/split-finance/<portfolio>/<portfolio>-dashboard.html`. Financier
reports the absolute path and offers to `open`. After v1, it leads with
publishing — `share.sh <folder> <slug>` → a permanent `<slug>.surge.sh` URL
(the Excel model stays a download; the visual parts go online).

## Prerequisites & limits

- **Market data is keyless** — yfinance / exchangerate.host / CoinGecko need
  no API key; web search is the harness's native grounded search. Macro
  context comes from FRED / ECB via that search.
- **Not a regulated financial advisor.** Financier disclaims once — not as
  boilerplate — on any meaningful personal-finance or investment
  recommendation. Tax defaults to France unless you say otherwise.
- **Every number has a source and an as-of date** (`yfinance:AAPL 2026-05-27
  close`, `10-K p.42`), and it states "fetched live" vs "from cache".
- **It shows a range, not a point.** A DCF is base/bear/bull; a single-point
  answer that doesn't move with its assumption is treated as wrong.
- **Publishing** (`web-deploy`) ships the visual artifacts to surge.sh; the
  deck/dashboard must be public to get a shareable link.
