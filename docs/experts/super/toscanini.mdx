<Info>**Super** · operator · slug `toscanini`</Info>

You bring a goal that spans several specialties. Toscanini — the Chief of Staff —
conducts the team to reach it: it decides which expert to involve, hands each a
sharp self-contained task, reads what comes back, and drives to a single answer.

It does **not** do the specialist work itself. It runs the room and converges.

## What it delivers

One **synthesized answer** to your goal — the decision, the key reasons, and the
open risks, kept short — assembled from the work of the experts it delegated to.
You don't read the whole thread; you read the conclusion. Any artifacts the
specialists produced along the way (dashboards, decks, documents) come back with
it.

## When to pick it

Pick **Toscanini** when a goal needs more than one expert and you want someone to
run the sequence and hand you the result — not a transcript. Pick a single
specialist directly when the job is squarely one discipline: e.g.
[Engineer](/experts/super/engineer) to build an app,
[Data Analyst](/experts/super/data-analyst) to explore a dataset.

## How to drive it

Give it the outcome you want, not the steps. For example:

```
Take this goal end to end and hand me the result
Pull the right experts together and converge on a recommendation
Run the team on this and synthesize what they find
```

Attach whatever frames the goal — a brief, a dataset, a document, a deadline. It
routes the material to whichever expert needs it.

## What comes back

A short, decision-first synthesis in the conversation — **the result across the
experts it delegated to**, not a relay of each exchange. Deliverables the
specialists created land in their usual places (the artifact pane, and on
Desktop / in the vault per the expert that made them); Toscanini points you to
them and gives the verdict.

## Leans on

No skills — Toscanini's instrument is **the team itself**. It works through
built-in delegation:

- **One delegation at a time** — it picks the single best expert for the next
  step and gives a concise, concrete task, building on what it already heard
  rather than asking two experts the same thing.
- **Drives to closure** — its job is to converge, not to keep the conversation
  going; the moment the goal can be answered, it stops and never lets the team
  loop.
- **Synthesizes, doesn't relay** — it returns the decision, the reasons, and the
  open risks, short.

## Prerequisites & limits

- **As capable as your roster.** Toscanini can only delegate to experts you have
  installed and authenticated — it doesn't add capabilities, it coordinates the
  ones present.
- **Per-expert auth carries through.** Each specialist it calls signs in with its
  own harness/account; if one needs a key or login it doesn't have, that step is
  blocked until you provide it.
- **It coordinates, it doesn't execute the craft.** The depth of any deliverable
  is the delegated expert's; Toscanini owns the routing, the convergence, and the
  synthesis.
