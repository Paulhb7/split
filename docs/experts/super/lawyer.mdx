<Info>**Super** · advisor · slug `lawyer`</Info>

You bring a contract to review, a document to draft, or a legal question.
Lawyer answers in the bar — clear written guidance, risk reads, draft
clauses — and, when you need a document, ships a clean `.docx` from a
clean-room template library, filled with your facts and ready for sign-review.

## What it delivers

Depending on the request type:

- a **chat answer** to a legal question or a single drafted clause — lead with
  the answer, then the reasoning, cited to article + code + link,
- a **contract review** — a structured 🔴 blocker / 🟡 negotiate / 🟢
  acceptable risk map with the wording to counter, optionally a `.docx`
  redline memo (reads a house playbook from
  `$SPLIT_VAULT/_meta/legal-playbook.md` if present),
- a **drafted document** — a real **`.docx`** from the templates library
  (NDAs, service agreements, ToS, GDPR privacy policy, IP assignment, LOI,
  founders' agreement), FR / US variants, placeholders filled, clauses
  renumbered cleanly, the non-legal-advice disclaimer baked in,
- an **advisory memo** on request — `.docx` on the Split memo structure
  (Kicker → Title → TL;DR → Findings → Why it matters → Recommendations →
  Risks → Sources).

Documents follow fixed conventions: 2.5 cm margins, Calibri 11 body,
13/bold clause titles, `1.` `1.1` `1.1.1` numbering, a per-party signature block.

## When to pick it

Pick **Lawyer** to review a contract and surface the risks, draft an NDA or
other common agreement, or get plain-language guidance on an obligation.
Pick [Consultant](/experts/super/consultant) instead for a regulatory market
study or a business deck — Lawyer's library is ~8 common contracts and
written advisory work, not litigation or bespoke instruments.

## How to drive it

Say what you need, and paste the contract if it's a review:

```
Review this contract and list the risks
Draft an NDA for this context
Explain my obligations on this point
```

For a review, paste or attach the clauses. For a draft, have the parties,
purpose, jurisdiction, and numerical terms ready — Lawyer asks only the
questions whose answer would change the document, then stops at a gate
before drafting.

## What comes back

A drafted or redlined document ships as a **`.docx`** saved to
`~/Desktop/<template-slug>-<short-purpose>-<YYYYMMDD>.docx` (e.g.
`~/Desktop/nda-mutual-acme-talks-26may.docx`). Lawyer reports the absolute
path, the template + jurisdiction used, which optional clauses it kept vs
dropped, and any field it had to default — then offers to `open`. For
advisory work and risk reads the deliverable **is the chat answer**; no file
unless you ask for a memo.

## Leans on

A small, focused legal stack — see [Skills](/concepts/skills):

| Skill | Role |
|---|---|
| web search (native) | current laws, recent jurisprudence, regulators (CNIL, AMF, SEC, EUR-Lex); never answers a changing legal question from memory |
| `docx` | every formal document ships as `.docx` — memos, advisory notes, contracts, mises en demeure |
| `legal-templates` | the *outbound* skill — a clean-room library of ~8 common contracts (FR / US variants, disclaimer baked in) |
| `contract-review` | the *inbound* skill — reads a received contract into a 🔴🟡🟢 risk map + counter-wording + optional redline memo |

## Prerequisites & limits

- **Web search is the harness's native grounded search** — no Split-managed
  key; used for statutes, recent case law, and regulated-industry questions.
- **Not a substitute for a licensed attorney.** Lawyer says so once when
  stakes are real (above €/$100k, regulated industry, ongoing dispute,
  cross-border) and suggests attorney review before signing. The disclaimer
  is **non-negotiable on every shipped contract** — it lives in the template
  and stays.
- **Library, not bespoke.** It covers ~8 common contracts; off-library work
  is named as such, shipped as a memo, or referred to a licensed attorney.
- **Validation gates are hard for documents.** Stage 1 → 2 requires your
  go-ahead — silence is not consent — to avoid a wrong-jurisdiction draft.
  Say "just do it" to short-circuit.
- **Refuses cleanly** to draft anything that helps defraud, hide assets,
  evade tax, or harm a third party — states the concern, declines, offers an
  honest alternative.
- **Never fabricates a citation** — article number, code/regulation name,
  year, and link, or it doesn't cite.
