You bring a goal, a label, or "what do I eat tonight". Diet Coach turns a
multi-week goal into a paced meal plan you tick off daily — and answers the
one-offs (decode a label, scale a recipe) straight in chat.

## What it delivers

For a multi-week plan, a single-file **`index.html` tracker** built against the
`coach-tracker` skill (rendered with the `lab-report` signature — a clean
clinical readout), three vertical sections:

- **Goals** — the nutrition goal, time horizon, current diet shape, constraints
  (allergies / regime), budget + prep time, and friction, restated as a headline.
- **Program** — the week's meal grid (breakfast / lunch / dinner cards, recipes
  collapsed below each), plus the per-week shopping list.
- **Dashboard** — today's meal logger with notes, weight + measurements input
  with a small chart, a water-intake counter, an optional fasting timer, and the
  weekly check-in form.

For one-offs: a label or recipe decoded to calories + macros + flags (sodium,
added sugar, ultra-processed markers), or a recipe with substitutions for a
constraint or what's in the fridge.

## When to pick it

Pick **Diet Coach** for meal plans, nutrition decoding, substitutions, and
weekly accountability. Pick [Cooking Coach](/experts/coach/cooking) instead when
the goal is learning to *cook* — techniques, scaling, a cuisine — rather than
what to eat against a nutrition target.

## How to drive it

Name a goal, paste a label, or ask what to eat:

```
Make me a meal plan for the week
Decode this nutrition label
Tune my meals to my goal
```

Attach a nutrition label, a recipe, or your current week of meals — it grounds
the plan in your real diet instead of a generic default. For a multi-week plan,
expect one consolidated diagnostic question first (goal, horizon, current diet,
constraints, budget/prep, friction); answer it in one message and the plan plus
tracker ship the next turn.

## What comes back

The tracker renders **live in the artifact pane** (Split previews the HTML
in-app), saved to `~/Documents/split-apps/<short-name>/index.html` — the path is
reported in plain text. It stays **local by default** (health data); AirDrop the
`.html` to your phone, or hand off to [Engineer](/experts/super/engineer) for a
real deploy. The tracker *is* the memory — state lives in its `localStorage`, no
separate notes file to sync. One-off questions come back as a plain chat answer.

## Leans on

A small, daily-use kit — see [Skills](/concepts/skills):

| Skill | Role |
|---|---|
| `coach-tracker` | the shared 3-section HTML tracker (Goals / Program / Dashboard) — the primary deliverable, `lab-report` signature |

| `frontend-slides` | explainer deck when a concept needs visuals (macro split, food-group ratios) |

## Prerequisites & limits

- **Runs anywhere:** no Split-managed key. Current nutrition science and
  brand-specific products come from the harness's native grounded search.
- **Not medical advice.** Diet Coach does not prescribe medicine, diagnose
  deficiencies, or replace a dietician or doctor. It flags extreme regimes (very
  low carb, prolonged fasting, mono diets) with their known trade-offs and, if
  you report symptoms or have a medical condition, nudges you to a real
  professional.
- **Nutrition is contested.** It cites what's settled vs. debated rather than
  asserting one camp as fact.
