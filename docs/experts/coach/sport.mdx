You bring a goal — a 10k, a first pull-up, a 100kg squat. Sport Coach turns it
into a structured training cycle you tick off daily: progressive overload,
deload weeks, periodisation, dosed to protect against injury.

## What it delivers

For a multi-week program, a single-file **`index.html` tracker** built against
the `coach-tracker` skill, three vertical sections:

- **Goals** — the training goal, deadline, current level, time budget,
  equipment, and friction, restated as a headline.
- **Program** — the full 4–12 week cycle, every session an expandable card
  (warm-up / main work / accessory / cool-down, time-boxed).
- **Dashboard** — today's session with a checkbox per set and an RPE field per
  lift, a PR-tracker per movement, a weekly volume chart, a deload-week
  countdown, and a consistency streak.

The dashboard renders against one of two signatures — `scoreboard` for race
builds and event countdowns, `cockpit` for strength blocks and HR/zone work
(never blended in one tracker). For one-offs: a form check, recovery advice, or
race-day fuelling — plus form notes (common errors, cues) for the lifts in scope.

## When to pick it

Pick **Sport Coach** for training cycles, progressive overload, session design,
and recovery. Pick [Diet Coach](/experts/coach/diet) instead when the lever is
nutrition rather than training load — pair them when a body-composition goal
needs both.

## How to drive it

Name a goal:

```
Design my training cycle
Plan my progressive overload
Adapt my session to my recovery
```

Attach your current routine, recent PRs, or a race date — the cycle is built
around your real volume instead of a generic 5×5. For a multi-week program,
expect one consolidated diagnostic question first (goal, deadline, current
level, time budget, equipment, friction); answer it in one message and the plan
plus tracker ship the next turn.

## What comes back

The tracker renders **live in the artifact pane** (Split previews the HTML
in-app), saved to `~/Documents/split-apps/<short-name>/index.html` — the path is
reported in plain text. It stays **local by default** (training data); AirDrop
the `.html` to your phone, or hand off to [Engineer](/experts/super/engineer)
for a real deploy. The tracker *is* the memory — state lives in its
`localStorage`, no separate notes file to sync. One-off questions come back as a
plain chat answer.

## Prerequisites & limits

- **Runs anywhere:** no Split-managed key. Current programs and brand-specific
  gear come from the harness's native grounded search.
- **Not a clinician.** Sport Coach does not diagnose physical conditions. If you
  report pain (not soreness — pain), it stops and recommends a physio; it won't
  sell shortcuts that risk injury.
- **Early gains are neural.** If you're new, the first ~6 weeks are mostly neural
  adaptation — strength jumps aren't "real" yet, and Sport Coach says so rather
  than overselling the curve.
