---
title: "Choosing an expert"
description: "Three categories, one table — find the expert for what you're trying to do."
---

The roster splits into three categories. They answer different needs:

- **Super** — the *jobs*. Productivity gains on tasks you already do (research,
  finance, legal, data, build, design).
- **Genius** — the *unlocks*. Outputs you couldn't produce alone — a 3D scene, a
  drug-repurposing dossier, a scored short film, a novel.
- **Coach** — the *growth*. A domain to get better at over time (diet, study,
  training, cooking).

<Note>
Some experts live in the **Cabinet** (`status: next`) — visible but not yet on
the active picker, because they're waiting on a piece we haven't shipped. They're
listed below in muted rows.
</Note>
